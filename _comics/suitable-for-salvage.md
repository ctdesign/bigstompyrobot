_Suitable for Salvage_ is the debut short comic from writer Craig Parker, and artist Chris Taylor.

In space, something is bound to go wrong on a ship. When it does, the Intergalactic Highway Repair corporation is there. It's a lonely, thankless job for their repair specialists. 

It almost makes you wonder what would motivate someone to do it...
